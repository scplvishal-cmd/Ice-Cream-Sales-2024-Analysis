# 🍦 Ice Cream Sales 2024 Analysis

An end-to-end sales, revenue, and profitability analysis of ice cream business across 4 major Indian cities (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru) for the year 2024.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Highlights

* **Total Revenue:** ₹77.89 Lakhs
* **Total Profit:** ₹34.97 Lakhs
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 44.9%
* **Units Sold:** 79,453 units across 1,000 transactions
* **Geographical Coverage:** Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Seasonality:** Peak summer months (March - June) generate **51.7%** of annual revenue.
2. **City Revenue:** Mumbai is the largest market, contributing **33.5%** of overall revenue.
3. **Flavour Performance:** Chocolate and Vanilla lead in total volume, while Kesar Pista earns the highest profit per unit (₹60/unit).
4. **Channel Sales:** In-Store sales account for 49.4% of total revenue, followed by Online Delivery at 37.3%.
